Fintech’s Next Moat: Building Distribution, Trust, and Customer Access

To What Extent Has Customer Access Become the Central Competitive Battleground in Indian Fintech

Harini Sugunuri
Independent Researcher

October 2026 · India · Independently published

Abstract

This white paper examines the growing strategic importance of distribution partnerships in Indian fintech. Drawing on industry examples, academic literature and regulatory developments, it explores how customer reach, trust, contextual product placement, engagement frequency and access to customer data influence competitive advantage. The paper argues that product capabilities alone are insufficient to sustain differentiation when financial services can be replicated and embedded within existing customer workflows. It examines partnerships involving fintech firms, banks, non-banking financial companies and digital platforms, considering both their commercial benefits and their risks. These include channel dependence, margin pressure, loss of customer ownership and fragmented accountability. The analysis concludes that sustainable value depends on combining trusted customer access with product depth, appropriate data governance and clear responsibilities across the partnership.

Keywords: Indian fintech, distribution strategy, embedded finance, customer ownership, platform partnerships, trust, competitive advantage
